Privacy Policy

Eternus · AEtherML · Last updated: July 2026

This document is a draft for client review. It should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before publication.

1. Who we are

Eternus provides luxury wedding photography and cinematography. This policy explains how AEtherML (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects personal information when you visit our website, submit an inquiry, or book our services.

2. Information we collect

We may collect:

  • Contact details — names, email addresses, phone numbers, and wedding dates you provide through our contact form.
  • Booking information — venue, event schedule, package preferences, and communication related to your celebration.
  • Meeting scheduling — if you book a consultation through our calendar integration, we receive appointment time and basic scheduling metadata.
  • Visual content — photographs and films captured during your event, which may include you, your guests, and your vendors.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, device information, and usage data collected through standard analytics or server logs.

3. How we use your information

We use personal information to respond to inquiries, prepare proposals, deliver contracted services, process payments, communicate about your wedding, improve our website, and—unless you opt out in writing—showcase work in our portfolio and marketing channels.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate our business, such as hosting, email delivery, calendar scheduling, payment processors, and cloud storage for media delivery. These providers are expected to handle data only for the services they perform on our behalf.

5. Images, films & portfolio use

Wedding coverage naturally includes identifiable individuals. By booking our services, you acknowledge that we may display selected images and films on our website, social media, and in promotional materials. If you require privacy restrictions for specific guests or sensitive moments, please discuss them with us before your event so we can document your preferences.

6. Cookies & analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences (such as language) and to understand how visitors use the site. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some site features.

7. Data retention

We retain inquiry and contract records for as long as needed to fulfill our services, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Raw and edited media may be archived according to the retention period stated in your service agreement.

8. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure; we encourage you to use strong passwords and protect access to delivered galleries.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain uses of your personal data. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: contact us through the form on our website or via the email address in your booking correspondence.
